# Predeclared continuation review

This operational rubric accompanies planning/PHASE07_08_PROTOCOL.json. It is fixed before training outcomes, not a record of completed ratings.

Six authored prompts and sampling seeds 17/29 form twelve paired tasks. Both conditions use temperature 0.8, top-k 40 and a maximum of 128 new IDs. Keep all twenty-four continuations. The paired packet assigns A/B labels using a reproducible ordering; identities are in a separate review key.

Rate grammar, prompt continuity, local consistency and repetition separately: 1 poor, 2 weak, 3 mixed, 4 good, 5 strong. Record reviewer identity/date and support each rating with an observation from the text. For repetition, a higher rating means less unhelpful repetition. A reviewer should avoid the identity key until ratings are complete and mark uncertain judgments rather than manufacture precision.

Grammar concerns local readable phrasing. Prompt continuity concerns whether the opening is developed. Local consistency concerns stable characters, objects and actions. Repetition concerns unhelpful recurring phrases rather than merely repeated function words. These dimensions need not agree.

Blank rating fields are pending, not zero. No human means, agreement statistics or human-quality conclusions are reported before actual human ratings exist. Assistant qualitative review must identify its reviewer role and cannot be described as an independent human study. The packet alone is preparation, not a completed review.

Likelihood uses all fixed final target events with the full vocabulary; decoding filters affect samples only. Surface lexical metrics do not substitute for coherence judgments. One paired training seed and a small convenience sample limit general claims.
